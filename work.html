We had six weeks before a funding round to overhaul the identity, site, and product UI as one system — with no downtime and a clean handover to the in-house team.

Lumen Labs — the platform, rebuilt properly

Lumen Labs' analytics dashboard still carried the DNA of its three-year-old MVP: a six-step signup, an 11-second load, and a support queue full of UX questions.

We rebuilt it from the information architecture up over ten weeks — new onboarding, dashboard, and design system — shipping incrementally behind feature flags.

What we did

01

Information architecture

Card-sorting with 22 users; click depth cut from 4.2 to 1.8.

02

Onboarding rebuild

Six steps to two screens with smart defaults; completion rose from 41% to 89%.

03

Design system

60+ accessible components with token docs the in-house team can extend.

04

Performance pass

Dashboard now renders in 1.2s, down from 11s.
