Our Story

Six years. One direction.

From a spare room in 2015 to a senior team of eleven today — the milestones that shaped how we work.

2015

Founded in a spare room

Daniel left Pentagram with a laptop, a folding table, and one client. The pitch: senior-only work, no junior delegation.

2018

From table to studio

Moved into a real building in East London. Hired Maya and Tom. Crossed ten projects shipped — and learned what a real process looks like.

2020

First industry award

Won "Site of the Day" for a Lumen Labs redesign. First time the work was recognized on an international stage.

2022

International clients

Started working with brands across three continents. Grew to nine people — and still zero account managers.

2025

Agency of the Year

Named "Small Studio of the Year" at the IDA. Crossed 140 projects shipped. Grew to eleven — and that's where we're staying.

What We Believe

Three things
we won't compromise on.

Every engagement runs on the same three values — no exceptions, no shortcuts, no matter the size of the client.

01

Strategy before pixels

We won't open Figma until the brief is signed off. Clear positioning makes every creative decision faster, sharper, and easier to defend in a boardroom.

02

Senior team, no relays

The people in the pitch are the people doing the work. No account managers relaying messages between you and the team. No juniors hidden behind a director's title.

03

Built to be handed over

Every system ships with documentation your team can extend. No black-box design that breaks the moment we walk out the door. That's the standard.
